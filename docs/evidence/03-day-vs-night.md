# The national day-versus-night gap

**The claim:** since 2019 the US added far more clean power to midday hours than to 3am
hours.

## The numbers, corrected

| | 2019 published | 2019 corrected | 2025 | added |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Midday (10am-4pm) | 178.1 GW | **174.8 GW** | 239.5 GW | **+64.7 GW** |
| Overnight (12am-6am) | 159.0 GW | **155.7 GW** | 173.4 GW | **+17.7 GW** |

Ratio: **3.7x** more clean power added to the average midday hour than the average 3am hour.

**Scale:** a large nuclear reactor is about 1 GW. So roughly 65 reactors' worth was added
to midday and 18 reactors' worth to 3am.

## Why 'corrected'

The published 2019 baseline includes a nuclear plant counted twice (see
`04-arizona-error.md`). Corrected removes 3,338 MW from the 2019 overnight figure and
3,334 MW from midday. **Quote the corrected number and say you corrected it.**

## The trap

The overnight carbon-free SHARE was 155,693/392,186 = **0.397** in 2019 and **0.396** in 2025. Essentially flat.

**Never say the share fell.** Clean output rose 17.7 GW; demand rose faster. Saying the
share fell without saying output rose is the easiest way to state something false using
true numbers.
