# What the companies actually claim

**The single most likely challenge: "do they really say 100% renewable?"**

No. Not one of them. Every claim below is the verbatim sentence from their own published
report, with the page number. Quote these. Do not paraphrase them.

## Alphabet (Google) (GOOGL)

> "Despite this, we again matched 100% of our electricity consumption with renewable energy purchases (on a global and annual basis)"

- **Source:** `google-2026-environmental-report.pdf`, page **4**

## The point

Every one of these says **matched**, and every one says **annual**. None says its
datacenters *run on* renewable power. That is an accounting statement, and it is accurate.
The physical question, what came out of the wire at 3am, is a different one and is what we
measure. The gap between the two is the project.

This is also why the talk scores are around 0.4 and not 1.0: each qualifier (*matched*,
*purchases*, *annual*, *global*) cuts the claim's scope.
